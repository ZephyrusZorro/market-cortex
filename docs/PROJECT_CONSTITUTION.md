# Project Constitution

**Authority:** [Phase 0 baseline](architecture/PHASE_0_BASELINE_2026-09-26.md), §§1–3 and 14. This living text may refine but never silently overturn it. Changes require a numbered ADR and migration plan.

Mission: help one owner observe markets, test and challenge ideas, preserve what was known at decision time, and learn from outcomes. Execution occurs externally. A truthful result can be **no edge**, **wait**, or **data unavailable**.

Invariants: no silent freshness claims; no future information in historical research; no fabricated probability; material claims trace to evidence; append corrections instead of rewriting history; incomplete coverage is labeled; results show costs/sample/baseline; local data and personal notes remain exportable and backed up. Data rights and permitted retention are source-specific even in a private tool.

Scope proceeds by [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md). Phase 1 contains only the market data foundation and read/health API. No autonomous trading, broker orders, strategies, advanced feeds or AI code. Engineering discipline: typed versioned contracts, reproducible fixtures, migrations, secure defaults, measured infrastructure, and owner approval at phase gates.
