## Sustainable free-data strategy

| Provider | Official/current evidence | Local conservative policy |
|---|---|---|
| Binance spot | Live `exchangeInfo`: 6,000 weight/minute, 300,000 raw requests/5 minutes; klines weight 2, max 1,000 rows | 30 GET attempts/minute, minimum 1-second spacing; one combined selected-symbol socket |
| Upstox | Standard history limits 50/sec, 500/min, 2,000/30 min | 40 attempts/min and 1,000/30 min, minimum 1-second spacing; one LTPC socket, app cap 50 keys |
| Alpaca Basic | Free IEX live, 30 subscribed stock symbols, 200 historical requests/minute; latest-15-minute historical restriction listed | 60 attempts/min, minimum 1-second spacing; one IEX socket, ≤30 symbols |

Sources checked October 14: [Binance REST spec](https://github.com/binance/binance-spot-api-docs/blob/master/rest-api.md), [Binance stream spec](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/binance/binance-spot-api-docs/master/web-socket-streams.md), [Upstox rate limits](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/rate-limiting/), [Upstox V3 feed limits](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-market-data-feed/), [Alpaca Basic](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api).

Upstox normal entitlement documents two sockets/user and up to 5,000 LTPC keys when that category alone is used; mixed-mode limits differ. The app deliberately caps at 50, not thousands. Binance has 1,024 streams/socket, five incoming control/ping/pong messages/sec, 24-hour connections and IP-level connection-attempt limits; two streams per selected pair count separately. Keepalive/reconnect handling must stay bounded. Alpaca live prices/volume represent IEX, not consolidated US market coverage; SIP/delayed-SIP requires a separate successful entitlement probe and explicit DELAYED labels.

[Upstox's free Analytics Token](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/analytics-token/) offers one-year read-only GET access to market/history/WebSocket categories without a static IP. Prefer it for future rotation. The successful receipt does not reveal whether the supplied Bearer token was an Analytics Token or its expiry; do not claim either. Alpaca credentials are protected locally, but are not inherently restricted to market data; application host/path GET allowlists and fixed WebSocket protocols enforce this scope.

Discovery downloads metadata, not 73,494 price histories. Refresh catalogs manually initially; reuse unchanged semantic snapshots and retain a small immutable source-check receipt. Changed metadata produces a new snapshot. History cache keys include provider/native ID/interval/feed/adjustment/source knowledge. Serve covered ranges locally; fetch only gaps. Empty responses retain a retryable receipt, not fabricated coverage. Existing finalization grace, pagination limits, dedup and revision/as-of behavior remain enforced.

For broad on-demand chart approval later: one foreground request at a time, ≤365 daily calendar days or ≤3 intraday calendar days, ≤20 pages/20,000 rows/request, a **512 MiB source-cache ceiling** and no automatic evidence deletion. This broader universe history scope is **not** implied by the seven-listing acceptance import. Independent archives now support a retained ceiling that cannot be relaxed by reopening; capacity exhaustion refuses publication and leaves prior evidence intact. New review bundles enforce 512 MiB. The production candidate must validate quota/error behavior before enabling unrestricted on-demand writes; existing independent roots were not retroactively modified to enable a quota.

Current request budgets are shared across transport instances **within one process** and count retries. They are not account-wide across programs. Deploy one service/worker per broker account, refuse duplicate workers, and account for other broker terminals/probes; do not run multiple independent credentialed services against the same account simultaneously. Stop/defer on local budget exhaustion or provider 429; respect numeric cooldowns. Do not stress-test free limits.
