## Remaining boundaries

- The pilot data is historical daily data ending with the last stored session in the connected catalog. It may be stale until the existing ingestion workflow refreshes it; the workstation does not call Binance directly.
- Upstox/Alpaca live authenticated validation, verified market mappings and calendars await credentials. Their read-only adapters remain intact. No India/US equity coverage is claimed.
- Native USDT prices are not converted to INR or USD because no governed FX series is installed.
- Browser-local watchlists and notes are not part of PostgreSQL/Parquet backup. Manifest selection is not restored after a browser reload; the view URL alone is not a complete research permalink. The picker lists the 30 most recent manifests.
- The full overview takes several seconds on the tested pilot machine. A future measured optimization may reuse loaded series inside the Phase 2 state composition or add context-keyed caching without weakening as-of semantics. This is a performance improvement candidate, not a correctness failure.
- Intraday feeds, options, broader universes, authenticated multi-user access, AI orchestration and Phase 4 features are outside this release.

The workstation is ready to be the UI foundation for Phase 4, subject to the above data and scope boundaries. [Setup and workflow](../workstation/PHASE_3_WORKSTATION.md) are documented separately.
