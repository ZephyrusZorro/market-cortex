## Scientific interpretation

Phase 12 N=1800 is dependent instrument/session target rows per completed study, not 1800 independent trades. Error skill is measured against the training mean; comparisons of different targets/horizons are not interchangeable. The fixed ensemble did not beat its baseline. Exploratory factor/importance/analogue reports are noncausal. Calibration diagnostics do not establish validated live forecasts. Search/holdout reuse is visible; unavailable statistical methods return reasons instead of significance values.

The canonical bar simulation and supplied risk shocks are separate from forecast-error metrics. A small positive in-sample simulation is not prospective profitable evidence. Promotion can only create a human-approved research baseline and cannot activate a detector or broker operation.
