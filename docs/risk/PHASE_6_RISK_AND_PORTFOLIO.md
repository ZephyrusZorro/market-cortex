# Phase 6 — Deterministic Risk & Portfolio Intelligence

MARKET CORTEX stores **local research positions** and calculates risk from pinned Phase 1 daily bars, Phase 2 features and Phase 5 theses. It never sends an order, synchronizes with a broker or treats a research size as an execution instruction. The current live data scope is Binance Pilot 5; Upstox and Alpaca remain `DEFERRED_NOT_CONFIGURED`.

## Portfolio and point-in-time contracts

`Portfolio` (`portfolio_v1`) has a UUID, owner label, capital, base currency, classification (`OWNER_RESEARCH` or `SYNTHETIC_TEST_PORTFOLIO`), version and timestamps. `PositionInput` has a UUID, canonical instrument UUID, positive long quantity and average cost, native currency, opening/closing times, optional thesis UUID, explicit invalidation and target, note and source type. Every add, update or close appends a complete immutable `portfolio_versions` JSONB snapshot with actor action, reason and recorded time. The `portfolios` row only advances a version pointer. `SYNTHETIC_TEST_PORTFOLIO` can be created only in a `PILOT_TEST` database whose name ends `_test`.

`RiskSnapshot` (`risk_snapshot_v1`, `portfolio_risk_daily_v1`) pins portfolio version, dataset manifest, UTC as-of, calculation source hash, observations and feature IDs, price and market-state freshness, coverage and warnings. It includes priced open-position value and unrealized P/L in base currency only when all positions have prices and no unsourced FX is needed; unrecorded cash is excluded. The risk engine resolves the newest portfolio version **recorded by the cutoff** unless an explicit older version is chosen. A requested future portfolio version or unpublished manifest fails closed. A saved risk snapshot is content-addressed, immutable and idempotent. A historical manifest can be evaluated at a later cutoff without admitting observations outside that manifest. A thesis invalidation is drawn only from a thesis version visible by the cutoff and in the selected manifest's ancestry.

The owner can create a portfolio and manually record positions through loopback-only APIs and the Portfolio & Risk workstation. No CSV parser or broker import was added. Position corrections and closures are explicit versioned actions. Thesis risk requests select a version recorded and evaluated by the cutoff; a later thesis head cannot hide that earlier evidence. Invalid long invalidation/target levels are rejected as request validation errors. The owner main database was not used for development or synthetic fixture insertion.

## Position risk, R and research sizing

For a long research position with positive quantity `q`, average cost `e`, stored current/reference price `p`, explicit invalidation `i < e`, and optional target `t > e`:

- Market value = `q × p`; cost basis = `q × e`; unrealized P/L = `q × (p − e)`; P/L fraction = `p/e − 1`.
- Entry `1R` = `q × (e − i)`; current R = `(p − e)/(e − i)`; target R and reward-to-risk = `(t − e)/(e − i)` only when `t` exists.
- Distance from the stored price to invalidation = `p − i`; current risk to invalidation = `q × (p − i)` only while `p > i`. A breached invalidation is marked unknown/breached, never reported as zero risk.
- The owner may supply an invalidation. A thesis may supply one only when it has a machine-readable, numeric close-below threshold on a thesis version visible at the cutoff. No moving average, stop or target is invented to complete a calculation.

Risk values are Decimal quantities rounded to eight places for display and persistence. The engine reuses Phase 2 `annualized_volatility(20)`, Wilder `atr(14)`, `volatility_percentile(20,90)`, `drawdown`, `maximum_drawdown`, `drawdown_duration` and `recovery_state` rather than implementing competing indicators. The feature values are contextual, not order instructions. Phase 2's 60-return same-session Pearson correlation supplies the correlation matrix. Insufficient alignment yields null with sample count and coverage, not zero correlation.

`risk-sizing` and `POST /risk/sizing` implement **research sizing** from either a fixed monetary risk budget or a fixed fraction of capital. Hypothetical quantity = risk budget / `(entry − invalidation)`; target R stays unavailable when no target is provided. Short positions, leverage, lot-size/exchange constraints and actual order quantities are outside Phase 6.

## Portfolio heat, exposure, concentration and BTC sensitivity

The Phase 6 heat convention is **sum of open long positions' current stored-price-to-invalidation risk / declared portfolio capital**. Entry 1R remains separately visible. Heat is unavailable if any position lacks price or valid invalidation, has breached its invalidation, or needs unsourced FX conversion. Per-position risk remains native-currency even then. Exposure retains instrument, crypto asset-class/native-currency, currency, thesis/strategy-link and Binance provider buckets. Gross and net directional totals are calculated only when every open position has a stored price and the native currency equals portfolio base currency. No USDT→USD or USDT→INR parity is assumed. Unavailable conversion is a warning, never an invented FX rate.

The explicit single-instrument concentration threshold is **35%** of measured gross exposure. A correlated cluster is flagged when a held pair has Pearson correlation at least **0.80** and combined gross weight at least **50%**; no causal or statistical-beta claim follows. BTC sensitivity is a **correlation-weighted exposure proxy divided by capital**, with BTC weight 1; it is not regression beta. It is unavailable if a required BTC correlation, price or currency conversion is missing. The 60-return window, pair sample count and manifest are retained. These thresholds belong to calculation version `portfolio_risk_daily_v1` and must be versioned when changed.

Portfolio drawdown is **unavailable** until a genuine portfolio equity series exists. Instrument drawdown fields come from their own daily close histories and must not be summed into a portfolio drawdown. VaR/CVaR are deferred rather than presented with unsupported precision.

Market-state freshness comes from Phase 2's `data_freshness` dimension independently of its regime classification. A stale source remains `stale` even when it makes the regime unavailable. Position and feature freshness remain pinned to the same observation cutoff.

## Explicit what-if scenarios and overlays

`ScenarioDefinition` contains a name, kind and bounded Pilot 5 symbol-to-fraction shocks. Instrument, broad-five-crypto and custom shocks are arithmetic **what-if assumptions**, not forecasts. Position P/L contribution is stored market value times its explicit shock; unchanged symbols contribute zero. Results expose each position, native-currency totals, base-currency portfolio P/L and change divided by declared capital only when FX and prices permit aggregation. Coverage and warnings remain visible.

A hypothetical thesis/position overlay computes before and after snapshots under the **same portfolio version, manifest and cutoff**, without mutating the portfolio. It reports exposure, heat, BTC proxy and optional scenario differences. If the added thesis lacks a price invalidation, incremental heat is unknown. The thesis detail and Portfolio & Risk workspace expose this path with explicit quantity and entry assumptions.

## APIs, CORTEX, privacy and limits

Loopback `/api/v1/risk` provides portfolio list/create/detail/version history, position changes, pinned risk calculation and optional immutable save, saved snapshot lookup, scenarios, overlays, thesis risk and pure sizing research. The CLI adds `portfolios`, `portfolio-risk`, `portfolio-scenario` and `risk-sizing`; its CORTEX `research` command accepts a portfolio ID and explicit scenario shocks. No endpoint accepts arbitrary SQL or invokes a broker. The frontend proxy allows only fixed route patterns to loopback FastAPI.

CORTEX's new allowlisted **read-only** `risk.portfolio`, `risk.scenario` and `risk.overlay` tools issue evidence IDs for calculated totals, positions, correlations, concentration, warnings, explicit scenario assumptions and hypothetical thesis deltas. The overlay needs a thesis ID, portfolio ID, explicit quantity and entry price; without those assumptions, an incremental-risk question fails closed. An optional invalidation and scenario shocks permit heat and stress comparisons. CORTEX can cite these values and challenge missing data; it cannot generate canonical money figures. Risk questions can select a sole local portfolio or specify its ID. If more than one portfolio exists, a portfolio ID is required. Existing cloud gating treats the question as private owner data and blocks cloud model invocation even if a broad cloud flag is true. The local deterministic fallback remains available. Portfolio records and risk snapshots stay in PostgreSQL and are covered by the existing encrypted backup of that database.
